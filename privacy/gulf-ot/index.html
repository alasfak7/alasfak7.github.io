Privacy Policy — Gulf OT

Overtime Tracker for Gulf Workers

by ALsoftrix

Effective Date: February 21, 2026  |  Last Updated: February 21, 2026

1 Introduction

ALsoftrix ("we", "our", or "us") built the Gulf OT app as a free, ad-supported application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our app.

This Privacy Policy complies with Google Play Developer Policies and applicable data protection regulations.

Key Point: Gulf OT is an offline-first app. All your overtime data is stored locally on your device and is never uploaded to any server.

2 Information We Collect

Data Stored Locally on Your Device:

  • Work schedule and overtime entries
  • Salary and overtime rate settings
  • App preferences (theme, display settings)

This data is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. We do not have access to this data, and it is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

Data We Do NOT Collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone)
  • Location data
  • Contacts or call logs
  • Photos, media, or files
  • Account credentials or passwords

3 Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

Service Purpose Type
Google AdMob Displaying advertisements Ads
Google Play Services App distribution & updates Platform

These services may collect information such as:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • App interaction data for ad personalization

For more information on how Google handles data, please refer to:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob & Privacy

4 Advertisements

Gulf OT is a free app supported by advertisements. Ads are displayed in the following locations:

  • Banner ads on the Home and Summary screens
  • Rewarded ads when exporting PDF reports (optional — watch to unlock)

No ads are shown on the Settings or Entry screens. You may see personalized ads based on your Google ad preferences, which you can manage in your device's Google settings.

Users can opt out of personalized advertising through their device's Google Ad Settings.

5 Data Security

We do not sell, trade, or rent user data to any third party.

Since all your data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your data is as secure as your device itself
  • We recommend using device-level security (PIN, fingerprint, etc.)
  • Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all locally stored data
  • We have no ability to recover your data if it is lost

6 Data Retention

All app data is stored locally and remains on your device until:

  • You manually clear the app data
  • You uninstall the application
  • You reset your device

We do not retain any user data on external servers.

7 Children's Privacy

Gulf OT is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is designed for working adults who need to track overtime hours.
