Privacy Policy

Last updated · 28 May 2026

Almeer Technical Services Co.WLL ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the Almeer Manpower employee app collects, how we use it, and how it is protected.

1.Information We Collect

Your name, Civil ID, passport, employee number, designation, nationality and mobile number; documents sent to you (payslips, certificates, letters); document expiry dates; your profile photo; and a device token used to deliver push notifications.

2.How We Use Your Information

Solely to manage your employment with Almeer — to deliver your documents and payslips, send document-expiry reminders, and provide secure access to the app. We do not use your data for advertising.

3.Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure, access-controlled servers, with files held in encrypted cloud storage. Passwords are stored hashed. Access is protected and logged.

4.Who Can Access It

Only authorised Almeer administrators. We do not sell or share your data with third parties, except where required by law or by Kuwait government authorities.

5.Data Retention

We keep your data for the duration of your employment and for any period required by law, after which it is securely deleted.

6.Your Rights

You may request a copy or correction of your personal data, or raise any concern, by contacting your Co-Ordinator.
